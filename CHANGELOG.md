# Changelog

## 0.1.0-beta.1 — 2026-10-21

- Android encrypted vault with master-passphrase and optional biometric unlocking.
- Service-only search, secret generation and encrypted manual import/export.
- Optional encrypted Google Drive or SFTP backups, durable retries and bounded history.
- Public-source preparation, privacy/recovery documentation and third-party inventory.
- SSH-style host-key confirmation, saved pinning and explicit changed-key approval.
- Show/hide controls for SFTP credentials and actionable, secret-free connection/file diagnostics.
- Fixed release-only SSH key-exchange failure caused by shrinking package boundaries.
- Published homepage, privacy policy, support guide and Google production branding.

Maintainer acceptance covers all app features, including successful manual, Google
Drive and SFTP backup/restore on the owner's Samsung/API 36 phone. Additional device
coverage, independent fresh-device/non-test-account conditions and an independent
security audit are not established by that report. See docs/PROGRESS.md for evidence.
