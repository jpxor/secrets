Your secrets. Your backup.

Store passwords, usernames and tokens in an encrypted vault on your phone. Use it offline, or back up to Google Drive or your own SFTP server.

Download for Android ↗Installation & recovery →

Store, find and copy.

Add and edit entries, search by service name, and generate passwords or tokens. Unlock with your master passphrase or optional fingerprint authentication.

Service names, usernames and secrets are encrypted with AES-256-GCM. Argon2id derives a key from your passphrase. No Secrets account is required.

Choose your backup.

Local only. Keep your vault on your phone and export an encrypted file whenever you need a backup.

Google Drive. Back up automatically to a private app folder in your Google account. Secrets does not access your other Drive files.

SFTP. Back up automatically to your own SSH server, with host-key verification.

Automatic backups upload encrypted data, even while the vault is locked. Check their status in the app; Android may delay background uploads.

BACKUP & RECOVERY

Restore your vault.

Restore from Google Drive, SFTP or an exported file using your master passphrase. Keep it safe: there is no passphrase reset, and fingerprints cannot recover your vault on another phone.
