Keep your service names, usernames and secrets encrypted on your phone. Choose local-only use, or send encrypted backups to your own Google Drive or SFTP server.

Pre-release · Recovery and broader device testing are still in progress. No independent security audit has been completed.

Simple by design.

One vault on one phone. Add, edit, search by service, generate a password or token, and copy when you need it. Unlock with your master passphrase or optional device biometrics.

Your master passphrase protects portable recovery. Biometrics help unlock this phone; they cannot recover a vault on a replacement phone.

Choose where
your backup lives.

Local only. Use the vault offline and export an encrypted file yourself. Keep a separate copy before replacing or resetting your phone.

Google Drive. Optional encrypted snapshots in the app's private Drive data folder. Google production access and branding are configured; authorization with a new user's account and fresh-device recovery still need validation.

Your SFTP server. Encrypted snapshots over SSH, with explicit host-key verification. You provide and maintain the server.

Automatic backups run within Android's scheduling limits. Check backup status and test recovery before relying on them.
