Recovery & support

To recover your vault, you need an encrypted backup and its master passphrase. There is no passphrase reset or recovery key. Fingerprint unlock works only on the phone where you set it up.

Install and update

Secrets requires Android 8 or later. Download the APK from GitHub Releases, open it on your phone and follow Android's installation prompts.

Back up before updating, then install the new APK over the existing app. Do not uninstall Secrets or clear its data to update.

Back up your vault

  1. Keep your master passphrase somewhere safe, separate from your backup.
  2. Connect Google Drive or SFTP and check for a successful backup in the app, or export an encrypted file to a separate location.
  3. Test recovery on a spare phone before you need it. Keep your original vault and backup until you have confirmed the restored records.

Android can delay automatic backups. Check the last successful backup before resetting or replacing your phone.

Restore on another phone

Choose restore during setup. Connect the same Google Drive account or SFTP server, or select your exported file. Enter the backup's master passphrase and check your records, then set up fingerprint unlock if you want it.

Import replaces the local vault; it does not merge entries. Export any existing local records you want to keep before confirming replacement.

Scope and security

Secrets manages one vault on one phone. Backups support recovery, not simultaneous editing across devices. The app does not include autofill, attachments or shared vaults.

No independent security audit has been completed. Encryption protects stored vaults and backups; it cannot protect secrets exposed on an unlocked, compromised phone.

Get help

Open a GitHub issue with your app version, Android version, phone model and steps to reproduce the problem using made-up entries. Issues are public: never include your vault, passphrase, passwords, provider tokens or private keys.

Report security vulnerabilities privately through GitHub's vulnerability reporting form.
